How I Work

My 4-Step Design Process

A clear, collaborative methodology that guarantees consistency, strategic focus, and exceptional final deliverables.

01

Discover

Understanding your vision, target audience, brand story, and aesthetic preferences through a structured creative questionnaire.

02

Concept

Translating the creative brief into initial sketch directions, typography pairings, color schemes, and visual moodboards.

03

Design

Refining the selected creative direction with meticulous vector craftsmanship, optical balance, and iterative client revisions.

04

Deliver

Preparing clean, organized final vector assets, print-ready CMYK files, dielines, and web RGB assets with full commercial rights.

About Me

Passionate About Visual Craft & Strategic Clarity.

Hello! I’m Alex Morgan, a dedicated Graphic Designer with over 8 years of experience creating compelling identities, packaging, and print publications.

Design Philosophy: Great graphic design is the intersection of thoughtful strategy and aesthetic rigor. I believe every brand mark and layout should possess clarity, purpose, and timelessness.
